Spiritual & Historical Significance

Day 1: Prana Pratishtha & Avahan (Sep 14)

Invoking the divine presence into the idol with sacred Vedic chants, marking the commencement of the 7-day festivities with devotion and community togetherness.

The Sacred Offering of 21 Modaks

Offering Lord Ganesha his favorite sweet delicacy, the Modak, which symbolizes the sweet reward of spiritual enlightenment, patience, and knowledge.

Community Cultural Bonding

Champions the spirit of unity and cultural expression through youth talent showcases, traditional folk dances, drama competitions, and spiritual bhajans.

Day 7: Visarjan Shobha Yatra (Sep 20)

The grand farewell procession and eco-friendly immersion ceremony, signifying the natural cycle of creation, return to the elements, and blessing for the coming year.
