Where Devotion Meets Tradition

Ganesh Utsav brings together families and neighbors in a joyous celebration of faith, unity, and gratitude. From the sacred installation of the Eco-Friendly Ganesha idol to the daily morning and evening Aartis, this 7-day festival offers moments of peace, reflection, and community bonding for all ages.

Sacred Moments of the Day

Morning Kakad Aarti

06:30 AM – 07:00 AM

Start the day with morning prayers and fresh floral offerings.

Nitya Pooja & Archana

07:00 AM – 08:30 AM

Daily family Archana slots and priest rituals.

Evening Grand Aarti

07:00 PM – 07:45 PM

Community evening gathering, deeparadhana, and prasadam.

Chant with Devotion

"वक्रतुण्ड महाकाय सूर्यकोटि समप्रभ । निर्विघ्नं कुरु मे देव सर्वकार्येषु सर्वदा ॥"

O Lord with the curved trunk and massive body, whose splendor equals a million suns, please make all my endeavors free of obstacles always.
